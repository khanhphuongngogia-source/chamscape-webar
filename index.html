My Son Sanctuary
UNESCO World Heritage · Quang Nam, Vietnam
My Son Sanctuary is a cluster of Hindu temples built by the Cham kings between the 4th and 14th century. Designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1999, it is one of Southeast Asia's most significant archaeological sites.
